Why This Asset

"205 keys of freehold Chang Klan frontage — the last holding of this scale on Chiang Mai's Night Bazaar, sitting inside the same district where Asset World Corp has committed over ฿25 billion. This is not a cash-flow trade. It is a position in land that does not come to market twice."
ASKING PRICE: ฿800MB
฿3.90M/Key · ~US$21.9M · 23.9% below full replacement cost · positioned on land scarcity, not on current operating cash flow

The Asset. A 14-storey, 205-key freehold hotel on 3,920 sqm of Chanote land directly on Changklan Road — one of the largest single freehold commercial holdings remaining on this frontage. Independently operated since 2004, currently trading at ฿1,700 ADR / 67% occupancy, with the physical building intact but overdue for renovation.

The Opportunity. Full renovation (~฿1.2M/key) and, over the medium term, redevelopment to a materially higher FAR than the current 3.59× would be required to fully justify this price on the numbers alone — which is why this offering is presented as a long-hold land position first, and an operating-hotel improvement story second. Buyers who move now secure the entitlement optionality before it is priced in.

The Market. Asset World Corp's ฿25 billion-plus Lannatique commitment sits inside the same district, alongside recent branded openings that have visibly lifted the corridor's profile. Large, clean-title freehold parcels of this size in this exact location are structurally scarce — most comparable land nearby is leasehold, fragmented, or already built to capacity. That scarcity is the asset's true ceiling, and it sits outside every one of the operating scenarios modelled in this document.
